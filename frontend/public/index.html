About KenyaWatch AI
Open-source AI anti-corruption platform for Kenya
HACKATHON 2026
🎯 The Problem
Corruption costs Kenya an estimated KES 500 billion annually — about 30% of the national budget. Procurement alone accounts for 60% of government spending. Manual audit systems cannot keep pace.
💡 Our Solution
KenyaWatch AI combines ML fraud detection, satellite verification, and citizen reporting into one real-time intelligence platform.
🏗 Tech Stack
BACKEND
Node.js + Express on Railway. PostgreSQL. Anthropic Claude API. Helmet + rate limiting security.
FRONTEND
Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS. Calls backend API. Deployed separately on Railway.
DATA
Kenya e-GP, PPIP portal, KRA iTax, Sentinel-2 satellite API, EACC database, citizen reports.
