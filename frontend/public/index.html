About KenyaWatch AI
Open-source AI anti-corruption platform — Daggy Techs, Kenya
v3.0.0
🎯 The Problem
Corruption costs Kenya an estimated KES 500 billion annually — 30% of the national budget. Public procurement accounts for 60% of government spending. Manual audits cannot keep pace with the scale and sophistication of modern procurement fraud.
💡 Our Solution
KenyaWatch AI combines ML fraud detection, satellite verification, and anonymous citizen reporting into one real-time intelligence platform — empowering EACC, journalists, and citizens simultaneously.
🏗 Architecture
BACKEND
Node.js + Express on Railway. PostgreSQL with 40+ real contracts, 10 ghost projects, citizen reports. Helmet + rate limiting.
AI LAYER
Anthropic Claude Sonnet for chat. Custom ML risk scorer, credibility scorer, satellite analyzer. All models trained on Kenya procurement patterns.
DATA
40 Kenya govt contracts, 10 ghost projects (satellite-verified), 10 citizen reports. Real supplier patterns, county data, market benchmarks.
